Privacy Policy

Moveall · Last updated: October 2026

🔒 In short

Moveall does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter — your name, scores, and settings — stays only on your own device. We don't have a server, we don't have accounts, and we don't see any of it.

What information Moveall stores

Moveall saves a small amount of information directly on your device, using your browser or app's local storage. This never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It includes:

  • The name you choose to enter (optional)
  • Your best scores for each game mode
  • Your selected theme, sound, and language preferences
  • Your current in-progress game, so you can close the app and continue later

What Moveall does not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal information
  • We do not have user accounts or logins
  • We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties

Advertising

Moveall shows ads through Google AdMob. You can read about what information AdMob collects and how it's used on Google's official page: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Sharing your score

Moveall includes an optional "Share Score" button. If you use it, your device's own share menu or clipboard is used to let you send a message to an app of your choosing (such as WhatsApp or Instagram). This action is entirely initiated by you, and Moveall does not see or record what you share or who you send it to.

Children's privacy

Moveall is a simple puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted on this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
